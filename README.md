# Earth Analog Explorer: Moon and Mars

**Developed by Veer Parmar**  
**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

## About the Project

Earth Analog Explorer is a project developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. It is based on the challenge of identifying locations on Earth that have environmental and geographical similarities to potential permanent Moon Base locations and regions on Mars.

The main idea behind this project is to explore how certain places on Earth can help us understand the conditions that astronauts might experience during future missions to the Moon and Mars.

Using an interactive globe, users can explore different parts of Earth. By clicking on a location, the platform helps identify nearby places that may have environmental conditions similar to those found on the Moon or Mars.

These locations can then be explored as potential analog environments for experimentation, research, and simulation on Earth.

## What Does the Project Do?

The project provides an interactive way to discover and explore Earth locations that may resemble certain environments on the Moon and Mars.

Its main features include:

- **Interactive Globe:** Allows users to explore different geographical locations around the world.
- **Nearby Analog Locations:** By selecting a location on the globe, users can discover nearby places that may share similar environmental conditions with selected regions of the Moon and Mars.
- **Environmental Exploration:** Helps users understand the similarities between certain Earth environments and extraterrestrial landscapes.
- **Earth-Based Experimentation:** Provides an opportunity to explore and simulate certain space-related environmental conditions using locations on Earth.
- **Space Research and Learning:** Makes the concept of planetary analog environments easier to understand through geographical exploration.

## Why Is This Important?

Building permanent human settlements on the Moon and Mars will require a better understanding of their environments and the challenges they present.

Although Earth is very different from these celestial bodies, some of its locations have environmental characteristics that can help researchers study specific aspects of extraterrestrial conditions.

For example, deserts, volcanic regions, polar areas, and other unusual landscapes can provide useful environments for studying certain challenges associated with space exploration.

By identifying and exploring these places, we can better understand how Earth-based research and experimentation may contribute to future space missions.

## Project Objective

The goal of this project is to connect geographical exploration on Earth with the future of human space exploration.

Through this platform, we aim to:

- Identify Earth locations that may serve as analogs for Moon and Mars environments.
- Explore environmental similarities between Earth and other celestial bodies.
- Make potential analog locations easier to discover.
- Encourage Earth-based experimentation and simulation.
- Support learning and research related to future human settlements beyond Earth.
